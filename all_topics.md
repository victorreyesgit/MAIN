# GitHub Topics

Every repo gets one `category-` and one `status-` tag.
A `published-` tag is optional: no tag means the repo is not published anywhere.

## Category

- `category-projects`: personal programming projects
- `category-research`: research work
- `category-playground`: experiments and tests
- `category-site`: the website itself (MAIN, Projects, Research, landing page)
- `category-other`

## Status

- `status-idea`
- `status-in-progress`
- `status-done`
- `status-abandoned`

## Published

- `published-projects`: listed on the Projects page
- `published-research`: listed on the Research page
- `published-other`: live on its own link, not listed on Projects or Research
